# IT Risk Register & Threat Assessment

## 1. Risk Assessment Methodology
This Risk Register evaluates the operational, technological, and compliance risks of the **Secure Banking System**. Risks are scored using a standard 5x5 qualitative matrix:

$$\text{Risk Score} = \text{Likelihood (1–5)} \times \text{Impact (1–5)}$$

- **Critical / High Risk (Score 15–25)**: Immediate management attention and hard technical controls required.
- **Medium Risk (Score 8–14)**: Technical controls supplemented by detective monitoring.
- **Low Risk (Score 1–7)**: Acceptable residual risk under routine operational monitoring.
